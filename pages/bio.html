Movie Marginalia is a project of A. M. Alcazar, a Madrid-born film and short story writer based in Manchester, England.

He was introduced to screenwriting in 2014 and years later he learnt TV comedy writing from Brent Forrester (The Simpsons and The Office). Inspired by the work of Roger Ebert and fellow film enthusiast, Daisuke Beppu, he began writing pieces that reflected on the insights produced by films and cinema as an art form. He hopes these pieces document a genuine point of view and further insightful conversation on cinema.
